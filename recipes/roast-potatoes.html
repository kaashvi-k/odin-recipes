Roast-Potatoes

Description

These are the most flavorful crispy roast potatoes you’ll make. And they just happen to be gluten-free and vegan (if you use oil) to boot.

Ingredients

  • Kosher salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon (4 g) baking soda
  • 4 pounds (1.8 kg) russet or Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled and cut into quarters, sixths, or eighths, depending on size (see note)
  • etc

Steps

  1. Adjust oven rack to center position and preheat oven to 450°F (230°C) or 400°F (200°C) if using convection.
  2. Meanwhile, combine olive oil, duck fat, or beef fat with rosemary, garlic, and a few grinds of black pepper in a small saucepan and heat over medium heat.
  3. When potatoes are cooked, drain carefully and let them rest in the pot for about 30 seconds to allow excess moisture to evaporate.
