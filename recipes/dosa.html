Dosa

Description

A dosa, dose, or dhosai is a thin, crispy South Indian pancake made from fermented rice and lentil batter. It can be served plain or filled with a flavorful potato masala, like the classic Masala Dosa.

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Lentils
  • Salt
  • etc.

Steps

  1. Soak 1½ cups raw white rice (Sona Masuri or dosa rice), ½ cup whole urad dal,1 tablespoon each of chana dal and toor dal, ½ teaspoon methi seeds together in water.
  2. Drain the soaked ingredients and transfer them to a blender. Add ½ cup water and grind into a smooth batter. Add more water, if needed, to achieve a medium-thick, pourable consistency.
  3. Add salt to taste, then gently mix the batter well with your hand. Mixing by hand helps incorporate air into the batter, making the dosa softer and lighter.
